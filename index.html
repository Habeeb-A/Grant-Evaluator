Evaluation Framework

Grounded in real EA evaluation frameworks.

Seven criteria, each with published anchors:

  • Problem stake: the magnitude this project addresses
  • Causal mechanism: activity to outcome — caps the rest
  • Execution plan: what, in what order, by when, with whom
  • Falsifiability: what would show it worked, or did not
  • Differentiation: why this will not otherwise happen
  • Resource coherence: ask, plan and people cohering
  • Downside profile: how it could make things worse

What it will not do:

  • Give an overall score. The bar is a funder's price, not a fixed standard.
  • Score your track record or team — it cannot verify either.
  • Score a criterion your document does not address.
  • Suggest stylistic edits, only changes to what you say or commit to.

Every score names the anchor it came from and quotes the passage behind it. If that quote is not in your document, the score is withdrawn.
